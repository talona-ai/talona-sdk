## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-15

- Add the initial TypeScript client for the Talona Browser API.
- Support browser create, get, list, and stop operations.
- Add typed errors, configurable timeouts, safe retries, cancellation, and custom Fetch support.
- Publish both ESM and CommonJS builds with TypeScript declarations.
